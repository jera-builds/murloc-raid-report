Crown of the Cosmos — Mythic killed 7/30

Season 1 is in the books.

v3 · 2026-08-09 · generated by the weekly refresh (interactive desktop run) · data through 7/30 (S1 Finale)

Crown of the Cosmos went down Thursday 7/30 — the S1 Finale night — kill pull at 8:19 after 4 wipes, closing out Midnight Season 1 at 7/9 Mythic (9/9 Heroic). It took 85 wipes across the final four Crown nights to get there: 25 on 7/21, 26 on 7/23, 30 on 7/28, and 4 more before the kill on 7/30. No raids have run since — this report is the season wrap-up, covering the 6 nights from 7/14 through 7/30 and clearing the 4-week reporting backlog in one pass. Output benchmarks were re-pulled at ilvl 293-295 (the guild's average 292.5 has crossed out of the July 290–292 bracket), so ceiling percentages this week aren't strictly apples-to-apples with July — see the data-quality panel below.

What "the bar" means: your blended median parse percentile ≥ 75 — literally performing better than 75% of players on your spec, on a typical pull (not your best one). The "throughput ceiling" number is different: it compares your best-kill DPS/HPS to the raw output of a 75th/95th-percentile player at ilvl 293-295 — a check on whether the ceiling exists, not whether you hit the bar. Never call the ceiling number "the bar."

Data quality notes this week

  • Killing-blow attribution is truncated on 3 of the 4 Crown nights. WCL caps the deaths pane at 200 rows/report; 7/21 (39% coverage), 7/23 (38%), and 7/28 (32%) only captured the first ~10–13 of 25–30 wipes each. 7/16 and 7/30 are complete. Every mechanic score and killing-blow count in this report is a likely undercount for those 3 nights — the true repeat-death rate on Crown is probably higher than shown.
  • Pulselmao, Sé, and Ultiwa attended none of the 6 new raid nights (7/14–7/30). Their potion and mechanic subscores are held at neutral defaults rather than scored against missing data.
  • Output benchmarks moved with the guild's gear. Benchmarks were re-pulled at ilvl 293-295 because the guild's average (292.5) crossed out of July's 290–292 bracket. Ceiling percentages this week are not strictly comparable to July's numbers.
  • Throughput ceiling uses median-across-bosses, not best-single-boss — see compute_notes.md for the full methodology and the sensitivity analysis behind that choice. A handful of burst/cleave DPS specs would read meaningfully higher under a max-across-bosses definition; both are recorded per-player for transparency.
  • Grimtacos' Heroic median uses his Frost spec, not Blood — his Blood spec has 0 logged Heroic kills this window. Mythic still reflects Blood, his primary tanking spec.
